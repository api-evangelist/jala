# JALA

> JALA is an Indonesian aquaculture technology company providing end-to-end solutions for the shrimp farming industry — connecting farmers, processors, and buyers through the JALA App for real-time pond monitoring and data analytics, the Baruni platform, farming supplies, harvest services, and traceable certified supply chains. Backed by 500 Global. This is an API Evangelist network profile; JALA runs an operator SaaS platform and does not publish a public developer API.
